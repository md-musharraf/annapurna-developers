Ranchi experiences significant temperature variations between summer heat (40°C+) and winter chills. Rigid materials like cement slurry and traditional black coal-tar (bitumen) have zero elasticity. As the concrete slab expands and contracts, these rigid coats crack instantly, letting monsoon water seep directly into the reinforcement bars. Annapurna Developers uses high-elongation elastomeric polymer membranes that stretch with thermal movement without tearing.
