Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our waterproofing methodology, pricing, warranty coverage, and civil works in Ranchi.

Ranchi experiences significant temperature variations between summer heat (40°C+) and winter chills. Rigid materials like cement slurry and traditional black coal-tar (bitumen) have zero elasticity. As the concrete slab expands and contracts, these rigid coats crack instantly, letting monsoon water seep directly into the reinforcement bars. Annapurna Developers uses high-elongation elastomeric polymer membranes that stretch with thermal movement without tearing.

Yes, absolutely. We provide a formal written warranty certificate with our company seal and registered signature by Ayush Mukherjee. The warranty covers waterproofing membrane failure, delamination, and water leakage under normal structural conditions.

In over 80% of residential cases, breaking tiles is NOT required! We use advanced non-destructive chemical injection and deep-penetrating nano-epoxy grouting that seals tile joints, trap collars, and submerged voids without demolition.
